Privacy Policy

AlphaBeta|Kids · Effective 15 August 2026

AlphaBeta|Kids collects no data whatsoever. It has no network code, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software of any kind. Nothing your child does in the app leaves the device it runs on.

Information we collect

None. There is no mechanism in the app by which information could be collected, because the app cannot reach the internet. We do not know who uses the app, how often, or what they tap.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers four settings so it looks the same next time it opens:

  • whether cards lead with the capital or the lowercase letter;
  • whether the blends and digraphs are switched on;
  • whether the deck is shuffled;
  • which card you were last looking at.

These are ordinary app preferences, held in the app's own private storage on the device. They are not personal information, they are not transmitted anywhere, and they are deleted along with the app if you remove it.

Children's privacy

This app is designed for young children, and we have built it so that using it is not a privacy decision at all. Specifically, the app does not:

  • collect, store, or transmit personal information from anyone, of any age;
  • ask for a name, email address, birthday, photograph, or location;
  • contain advertising, sponsored content, or in-app purchases;
  • include analytics, tracking, fingerprinting, or advertising identifiers;
  • include social features, messaging, or user-generated content;
  • link out to a website, store, or any other app.

Because no personal information is collected from children — or from anyone — there is nothing for a parent to review, correct, or delete, and no consent for us to obtain. We believe this satisfies the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and comparable rules elsewhere by simply not doing the things those rules govern.

Third parties

There are none. The app bundles no software development kits, no frameworks beyond Apple's own, and no services. The only third-party component of any kind is the Andika typeface from SIL International, which is a font file compiled into the app — it is not code, it runs nothing, and it communicates with nobody.

Data sharing and sale

We share nothing, sell nothing, and disclose nothing, because we hold nothing.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change. The full history of this page is public in the app's repository, so any revision can be inspected and compared.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at the app's public repository: github.com/jhoedeman/AlphaBetaKids/issues.
